Match Stats — Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 August 2026

Match Stats does not collect, transmit or store any personal information.

There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind, and no third-party services. The app does not send your data anywhere, because it does not send anything anywhere.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter — games, team sheets, player names, coded events, your club badge and your settings — is stored on your own device, by the app itself. None of it reaches us and none of it reaches anyone else.

Backups you create are ordinary files saved wherever you choose to put them, such as the Files app or iCloud Drive. Those copies are yours; where they go and who can see them is entirely under your control.

Camera and photo access

The app asks for the camera and photo library for two features, both of which run entirely on your device:

  • Club badge — you pick an image, and the app reads its colours to work out the app's accent colour.
  • Team sheet import — you photograph a printed team sheet and the app reads the names off it.

Both happen locally. The text recognition is built into the app; no photograph is uploaded, shared, or kept anywhere except in your own saved data if you choose to set a badge.

You can decline either permission and keep using everything else.

Local network

The macOS version can serve the app to an iPad on the same Wi-Fi network, so you can code a match on a tablet. That connection is between your own two devices on your own network. Nothing is sent to the internet, and the feature does nothing unless you start it.

Children

Match Stats is a tool for coaches and team staff. It collects no information from anyone, including children. Player names typed into a team sheet stay on the device they were typed on.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date at the top. Since the app collects nothing, any change is likely to be clarification rather than substance.
