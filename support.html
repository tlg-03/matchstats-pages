Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything is built into the app — there is no server, and no part of coding a match needs a connection. You can use it at a ground with no signal at all.

Where are my games kept, and are they safe?

On your device, in the app's own storage. The app also writes an automatic backup for itself as you go, which is included whenever your device backs up to iCloud or a computer.

For a copy you control — one that survives a lost or wiped device — use Settings → Backup all and save it to Files or iCloud Drive.

How do I move to a new iPad or iPhone?

  1. On the old device: Settings → Backup all, and save the file somewhere you can reach from both — iCloud Drive is easiest.
  2. On the new device: Settings → Restore backup, and pick that file.

Games are not transferred automatically, so do this before you retire the old device.

Can I put my club's colours on it?

Settings → Club, then add your badge. The app reads the badge and works out its own colours from it, so the whole app takes on your club's look.

Can I change the text size, or use dark mode?

Text size follows your device setting — change it in iOS Settings → Display & Brightness → Text Size and the app follows.

For light or dark, use Settings → Theme in the app. Auto follows your device. Printed reports always come out light, whichever you pick.

Something's wrong with a match I've coded

Most things can be corrected after the fact — tap an entry in the coding log to edit or delete it, and undo is available while you're still coding. If a match looks wrong in a way you can't fix, email us before deleting it; it's usually recoverable.
