Match coding for coaches, one app per sport

Need help? Email matchstatssupport@gmail.com

Tell us which app and which device you're on (iPhone, iPad or Mac), and what you were doing when it went wrong. If it's about a specific match, the date and the two team names help us find it.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The apps contact no server, ours or anyone else's, and no part of coding a match needs a connection. You can use them at a ground with no signal at all.

Where an app offers live share on game day, it uses the venue's Wi-Fi to reach coaches' devices directly. It still needs no internet.

Where are my games kept, and are they safe?

On your device, in the app's own storage. As you work, the apps also keep an automatic backup on the same device: a dated copy that is updated every few minutes while you make changes, with the twelve most recent files kept.

On iPhone and iPad it is in the Files app, in the app's folder under Backups. It is also part of your device's iCloud or computer backup, if you have one turned on. On a Mac it is inside the app's own folder in your user Library, and part of your Time Machine backup if you use one.

For a copy you control, one that survives a lost or wiped device, use Settings → Backup all. On iPhone and iPad, choose where it goes, such as Files or iCloud Drive. On a Mac it opens a save window so you can pick any folder, then asks whether the backup saved. Backing up and restoring are also in the File menu on a Mac.

How do I move to a new device, or between an iPad and a Mac?

  1. On the old device: Settings → Backup all, and save the file somewhere you can reach from both. iCloud Drive is easiest, and it works on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
  2. On the new device: Settings → Restore backup, and pick that file.

The backup file is the same on every device, so an iPhone or iPad backup restores on a Mac and the other way round. Restoring merges the backup into what is already there and deletes nothing. Where a game is on both, the copy edited last is kept, so restoring the same file twice won't duplicate anything.

If you set up a new iPhone or iPad from the old one's iCloud or computer backup, your games come with it. In any other case, use the steps above before you retire the old device.

Can I put my club's colours on it?

Settings → Add your club (once you've named your club, the row shows its name) → Choose badge (Change badge if one is already set). On iPhone and iPad you pick a photo; on a Mac you choose an image file. The app reads the badge and works out its own colours from it, so the whole app takes on your club's look. You can still adjust App colour and Button colour yourself before you Save.

Can I change the text size, or use dark mode?

On iPhone and iPad, text size follows your device setting: Settings → Display & Brightness → Text Size. It grows up to a point, so the very largest accessibility sizes don't make it any bigger. On a Mac the apps have no text-size setting. For more magnification on any device, use the system's own Zoom in its Accessibility settings.

For light or dark, use Settings → Theme in the app: Auto, Light or Dark. Auto follows your device. Printed reports and PDFs always come out light, whichever you pick.

Something's wrong with a match I've coded

Most things can be corrected after the fact. On the coding screen, the Coding log button (the list icon) lists the actions you've coded, newest first. Tap one (click, on a Mac) to open its editor, where you can change it or Delete it.

While you're coding, the Undo and Redo buttons are in the top bar. On a Mac you can also use ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z. The undo history ends when you leave the match.

Deleting a whole game can't be undone in the app. It can be brought back with Restore backup from a backup made before it was deleted: one you saved, or an older automatic copy. The newest automatic copy is updated as you work, so it may no longer have the game. If a match looks wrong in a way you can't fix, email us before deleting it and we'll help.

Live share: coaches can't find or join the match

Where an app offers live share on game day, check these in order:

  1. The analyst has gone live: in the match menu (⋯), Go live to coaches, with a PIN set.
  2. The coach's device is in Coaches view. The app asks how the device is being used each time it starts; if it is already in Analyst view, use Settings → Switch to Coaches view. The analyst's own games are under Analyst view.
  3. Both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network. Live share never uses mobile data.
  4. Both devices allow Local Network access where the system asks for it. On iPhone and iPad that is Settings → Privacy & Security (Privacy on iOS 15) → Local Network. On a Mac with macOS 15 or later it is System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network; earlier versions of macOS don't ask.
  5. If the match still isn't in the coach's list, the analyst taps the Live chip in the top bar to see the device's address. The coach chooses Enter the analyst's address and types it in.

If the coach is told the PIN is wrong, check it with the analyst. If it says the analyst is not letting coaches in just now, the session is full or pausing after wrong PINs. It keeps trying on its own. If it says the match needs a newer version, update the app on both devices.
